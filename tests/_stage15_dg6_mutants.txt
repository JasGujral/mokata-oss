DECLARED, NOT DISCOVERED LATER — what this guard does NOT do.

  It is a TEXT check over `docs/`, not a proof that the docs describe the shipped wiring. It can
  only ever catch this ONE falsified premise being re-asserted, because that premise is the one
  thing it knows a keyword for. A page that describes the hook route wrongly in some new way
  passes it completely. That is the honest bound: DG-6 closes the door this particular claim went
  stale behind, and claims no more.

  It also cannot see a page that asserts the premise across a LINE BREAK — "cmd.exe completes the
  extension-less path" on one line and "via PATHEXT" on the next. The scan is line-then-clause, so
  the mention and its negation must share a physical line. Left as-is deliberately: joining lines
  before scanning would make the clause split meaningless again (a paragraph would become one
  clause) and hand back the exemption M01 was raised to remove. Named here rather than omitted,
  because the difference between "we did not think of it" and "we thought of it and chose" is the
  whole value of the list.
